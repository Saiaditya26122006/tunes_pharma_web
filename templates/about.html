About Us

Welcome to Tunes Pharma, an exceptional pharmaceutical enterprise. Tunes Pharma was established in 2021, as the marketing division of Brinda Medicals.

We manufacture and market a large basket of pharmaceutical formulations across chronic therapeutic areas such as cardiology, diabetology, neurology, nephrology and dermatology. We provide high-quality medicines, trusted by healthcare professionals and patients. We are driven by a commitment to innovation, improved access and patient care. Our unwavering commitment to a diverse range of stakeholders, including patients, healthcare providers, and institutions, has led us to stand out in the market.

Our Story & Purpose

CHRONIC CARE & HEALTHCARE ECONOMY

Commitment to Health & Productivity

Since our inception, Tunes Pharma has been committed to providing high-quality, affordable medicines, with a strong focus on chronic care. In emerging economies, maintaining good health is essential not only for individuals and families but also for sustaining economic productivity. The growing burden of lifestyle-related disorders demands continuous innovation, adaptability, and new approaches to healthcare.

THERAPEUTIC REACH & RESPONSIBLE GROWTH

Evolving Healthcare Impact

With our industry experience and a strong commitment to our purpose, we aim to make a meaningful impact on the evolving healthcare landscape while building a sustainable and responsible business. Our presence spans key therapeutic areas, including Cardiology, Diabetology, Endocrinology, Nephrology, Neurology, and General Medicine.

Our Journey

Milestones & Growth

From a vision rooted in quality pharmaceutical care to a growing presence across India — every milestone reflects our commitment to excellence.

Oct 2021
Foundation

Tunes Pharma incorporated in Guntur, AP with ₹15L capital and a mission for quality, affordable pharma.

2022
Product Launch

Launched Ecoglim, RESGABA-NT, NACTaid & RABISHIR-DSR — entering AP & Telangana markets.

2023
Market Expansion

Launched in 4 districts, engaging 20+ KOLs per district to build prescription base and brand trust.

2024–25
Growing Traction

Organic commercial turnover: ₹1.48L → ₹2.78L → proving the model with zero investor funding.

2025+
PAN India Vision

Expanding beyond AP & Telangana across Cardiology, Diabetology, Nephrology and General Medicine.
